# How to Use these Brazilian Coffee Bags

A visual guide as I give the same bags to different people.

Takes a bit but replicates the "real thing".

<img src="../images/brazilian_instant_coffee_howto/01-coffee.jpg" alt="step 1" style="zoom:33%;" />

There is a small cut top right, cut linearly there.

<img src="../images/brazilian_instant_coffee_howto/02-coffee.jpg" alt="step 2" style="zoom:33%;" />

Then looks like this.

<img src="../images/brazilian_instant_coffee_howto/03-coffee.jpg" alt="step 3" style="zoom:33%;" />

Hold these white "holders", these will go to the glass, stabilise them.

<img src="../images/brazilian_instant_coffee_howto/04-coffee.jpg" alt="step 4" style="zoom:33%;" />

Cut the top, says "open" - oddly enough in 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁥󠁮󠁧󠁿.

<img src="../images/brazilian_instant_coffee_howto/05-coffee.jpg" alt="step 5" style="zoom:33%;" />

Attach to a cup like this so that water can be poured.

<img src="../images/brazilian_instant_coffee_howto/06-coffee.jpg" alt="step 6" style="zoom:33%;" />

Pour little bits of boiling water. Can be 2x max 3x times. At that point the powder becomes "cemented" with more not being able top pass through.

<img src="../images/brazilian_instant_coffee_howto/07-coffee.jpg" alt="step 7" style="zoom:33%;" />

That's it. Can wrap everything up for the bin/recycling  bin.
